About

Few subjects are discussed as often, and understood as little, as the Jewish people and the land of Israel. Headlines arrive without their history. Opinions arrive without their background.

Understanding Jews & Israel, by Bruce Galloway and Jennifer Bawden, follows the story from the kingdoms of the ancient Levant through exile, empire and diaspora, to the founding of the modern state. This sketchbook is the short version: nine plates, nine turning points, drawn in ink and a little colour.
